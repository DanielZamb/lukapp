Every other screen, same Lukapp

D3 settled the seven core screens. This pass takes the same decisions to every other screen: intake, alerts, budgets, savings recovery, month close, places, settings, categories, setup and access. Each flow is shown step by step at fontScale 2.0 in light and dark. The first step of each flow is also shown at 1.3.

Round 3Nav eyebrows (the grey label above a screen title) are capped at 1.3, so they stay clearly smaller than the title. Sort and filter is one filter at a time, here and on D3's History.

Round 2Your 28 comments are applied. Short status lines keep their 1.3 size at 2.0, space sets the hierarchy instead of cards and pills, options get the full width, context moves to the camera island, and Notification intake is rebuilt from the APK. See “Your comments” below for each one.

Rows

Category with its emoji, then the money on its own line, then one status line. Status lines keep their 1.3 size at 2.0 and stay on one line; the full text is in the screen-reader label and on the next screen.

Space, not cards

A tinted card only where you enter or decide a number (Post, Stay under, Move money, the History and Overview totals). Elsewhere the main number sits on the page and space sets it apart. No decorative pills.

Options and actions

Options get the full width, one per row, with a tick. The main action is pinned. Tall forms are pages; pickers and short decisions stay sheets.

Context

Facts you need while scrolling, like June's spending or a live category look, go in the camera island, or a docked pill without a centred cutout. Never a pill at the top of the page.
