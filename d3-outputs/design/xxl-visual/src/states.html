The sheets and states in between

D3 drew the seven core screens and D4 every other flow. D5 covers what was still missing: the sheets those screens open, the phone checks around sign-in, and the error, empty and loading states. Same rules, nothing new to learn. Each state is shown at fontScale 2.0 in light and dark; the first of each group is also shown at 1.3.

Same system

Rows are category, money, one status line. Status lines and eyebrows are capped at 1.3. Options get the full width. The main action is pinned.

Sheets or pages

Pickers (place, date, category, currency, time zone) and short confirmations stay sheets. Anything you type into at length is a page, with its context in the camera island.

Errors say what's safe

One red line that says what happened and that nothing is lost, then the retry as the pinned action. Amber for “not yet”, red only for “didn't work”.

Copy

The app's own strings, shortened where they ran past two lines at 2.0. Every change is listed under “Copy changes”.

Decisions

    Content. Real where the captures have it: the balance, the categories, the movements and the places. Examples elsewhere, marked in each state: the over-limit bell, the posting errors, the setup error, the access states.
