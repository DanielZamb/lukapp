The reservation rule uses all-time progress for activation. A non-cancelled plan reserves its expense only while its target remains unfinished, including when its source is suspended. With no unfinished plan, new principal equals the expense principal minus valid recovered principal across all historical plans. A released amount waits while the replacement is unfinished. Completed plans count their full frozen principal in this subtraction. If a completed plan later loses a contribution after a newer plan starts, it reopens and can coexist with the newer unfinished plan. Their frozen principals cannot exceed the expense principal under these commands: every completed predecessor's full principal was already subtracted when the newer plan was activated.
