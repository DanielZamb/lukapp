LUKAPP / CONNECTING SCREEN · D1 Design options · sample data · no build

From sign-in to Home, one screen

Today the app passes through six separate waits between sign-in and Home: the brand splash, then up to five bare spinners. Each option below keeps the purple splash on screen the whole time, swaps the spinner for motion and a status line that follows the real steps, and fades into Home only when the balances are ready. All three use only the brand images, plain views, opacity and transforms (react-native-reanimated, already installed). No new packages.

Network Motion Theme Text Loop

Steps and copy

  • protection: “Unlocking Lukapp…” (local protection check)
  • session: “Opening your session…”
  • account: “Connecting to your account…” (Clerk + Convex auth, owner check)
  • device: “Checking this phone…” (device access)
  • data: “Getting your lucas…” (first Home balances)

Rules for every option

  • Logo, mark and status line never move between steps; the animation clock doesn't restart.
  • Each status line stays at least 600 ms, so fast steps don't flicker.
  • After 8 s in one step: “Still connecting…”. In the device step, “Check again” and “Sign out” appear then (the demo uses 4 s).
  • Screens that need the user (biometrics, naming the device, access unavailable, errors) are not covered.

Accessibility

  • The status line is a polite live region; each step is announced once. The mark is decorative.
  • Reduced Motion: no breathing, rings or pulses; the status still updates; Home appears with a short fade.
  • XXXL: promise capped at 1.25×, status and footer grow freely, mark shrinks slightly so everything fits on a 320-pt screen.
