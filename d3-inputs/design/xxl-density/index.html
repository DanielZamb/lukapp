DirectionUseful changeTradeoff
A · Rhythm only8 inside a row, 16 vertical padding, 24 between cards, 32 between sections, 12 heading-to-content.All information stays visible. History still has 53 movements and grows when full type and two-fact rows are enforced.
B · DisclosureFirst four movements in two cards. Older dates and secondary metadata open on demand. Empty Drafts becomes a status disclosure.Extra taps. Counts and the route to all activity must stay visible. Essential money, dates, access warnings and retry state cannot disappear.
C · Disclosure and structureHistory starts with entries. Sticky dates retain context. Post and its batch editor keep a full-width action above the keyboard. Home routes long lists to their own pages.47 non-transfer movements need one tap each for category/place. Transfers retain viewing-place context. Pinned bars cost reading space. Focus needs testing.

Recommended combination

Use A's spacing everywhere at XXXL, B's bounded previews on Home and Overview, and C's persistent action for Post. Keep History's full page available, with a compact summary disclosure and sticky dates. Do not replace the default tier.

The balance is shown in the existing money style at a fixed, legible size. The real value, COP 56.526.163,06, does not fit the spec's 53 px hero at 288 dp. This needs an explicit MoneyHero presentation decision, rather than another shrink-to-fit rule.

The before images include the dev client's floating Tools gear and clipped native content. Those are evidence, not proposed UI. After layouts preserve one column, at most two items per card, labels above values, separate money lines, 56 dp targets, and uncapped body text.
