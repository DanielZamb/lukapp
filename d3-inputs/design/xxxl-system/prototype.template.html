Material

The pill now lives above the sheet, at the top of the phone. Scroll a sheet down to make it drop in. Tap it to go back to the summary. Swipe it up to hide it. Replay runs the entrance again.

Outside the sheetThe pill is a system-level layer, like a push notification. The sheet keeps all its height for rows, and the close button stays clear.

Two facts at mostName + one amount when collapsed. Expanded forms show two figures at most, following the XXL two-per-group rule.

FallbacksReduce transparency → Solid. Reduce motion → fade with no spring. Screen readers get one button: “Back to summary, Food & drink, COP 80.000 left”.

Same pill, four materials, over moving content. Hover to pause; you can also scroll it. Watch the rim: Liquid glass bends what is under its edges, while v1 just blurs it. Move the pointer to shift the highlight.
