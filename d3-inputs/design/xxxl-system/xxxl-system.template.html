01Overview

A layout tier, not a bigger copy. At XXXL the risk isn’t “too dense” anymore. It’s that a screen stops making sense: amounts break mid-number and cards grow taller than the phone. Each rule keeps one decision readable at a glance. Colours don’t change; space, grouping and line breaks do.

2Items per groupA third item starts a new group 40 pt below, or becomes “View all”.
2Facts per rowTitle + one value. Shared facts, like the date, move up to the group heading.
↵Money on its own lineNever beside its label. Currency goes above the hero amount.
1ColumnNo 2 × 2 tiles or side-by-side stats. Every control is at least 56 pt tall.
