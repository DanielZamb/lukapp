# Laws of UX

Use the [Laws of UX](https://lawsofux.com/) catalog as a set of hypotheses about how people perceive and use interfaces. The laws help explain a concrete design choice; they are not a checklist or proof that a design works.

## Apply the principles

1. Identify the user's task, audience, device, and the interface or flow under discussion. Inspect the supplied design, code, screenshots, or behavior before calling something a problem. If no artifact exists, frame recommendations as design options.
2. Find the decision that matters: choosing, finding, understanding, acting, waiting, recovering, or finishing. Use [the decision guide](references/decision-guide.md) to select a few relevant principles. Read the linked Laws of UX pages when their details or caveats affect the recommendation. Check the live [catalog](https://lawsofux.com/) when a relevant principle is missing from the guide or currency matters.
3. Explain the causal link: what a person encounters, why the principle suggests friction or clarity, and what to change. Include an observable way to check the change, such as a task attempt, error rate, time to act, or accessibility inspection. Prioritize by user impact and evidence, not by the number of laws cited.
4. Resolve conflicts in context. For example, fewer visible choices can ease an urgent decision but hide options that expert users need. Preserve the user's goals, necessary complexity, and accessibility. Treat any numeric claims on the site as context-dependent research findings, not universal UI thresholds.

For critiques, report only issues supported by the artifact or user evidence. Link each cited principle to its page and distinguish observed behavior from a predicted effect. For design or implementation, make the requested artifact; use the principles to guide specific decisions and verify the resulting flow. Keep the explanation proportional to the task.

Source: [Laws of UX](https://lawsofux.com/) by Jon Yablonski. This skill paraphrases selected ideas and points to the site's current pages for detail.
