# Lukapp mobile branding

Canonical SVGs copied unchanged from lukapp-marketing/assets/brand at commit 95b6df2868cbb4bd5ba2c099428788ca708af611. The approved mark is the curled L, firma, with its small gap. The custom wordmark remains vector lettering.

Icon geometry and violet finish follow finance-tracker-v2/design/logo-portfolio/src/aire-study.tsx. Startup uses its violet and night presentations, stacked signature and original Spanish brand copy. Product color tokens remain unchanged.

Run pnpm brand:generate from the repository root to regenerate PNG assets. Native launcher and splash resources come from Expo app.json and expo-splash-screen during prebuild. Android uses a centered symbol within the system splash mask; the startup screen supplies the stacked wordmark and copy.
