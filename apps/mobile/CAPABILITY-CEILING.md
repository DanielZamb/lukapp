## First-cutover boundary

Personal and Household with one Owner are supported scope. First-run implies Personal and creates My money silently. Business activation and collaborative invitations are deferred. Expo on iOS and Android is the first production client. TanStack web, public store launch, general-purpose offline synchronization, and a mobile-specific ledger are outside this core map.

Keep Local Entry Drafts and Notification Intake Candidates encrypted and device-local with their separate ownership and 15-day ceiling. There is no offline financial read model or automatic local posting queue. Deliberate online submission uses current authorization and the ordinary command. Clerk is the sole auth authority under [the auth map and ADR-0080](https://linear.app/sennkai-labs/issue/SEN-62/wayfind-the-auth-spec). This contract grants no additional access or shipping permission.

GAS remains authoritative. A source port, local checkpoint, simulator result, or injected-input experiment does not establish reconciled cutover, production readiness, physical-device proof, or native notification capture. Later reconciliation, rollback, and cutover evidence remain required under SEN-30.
