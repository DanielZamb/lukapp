# First-cutover UX contract

This is the implementation record for [SEN-31](https://linear.app/sennkai-labs/issue/SEN-31/freeze-the-first-cutover-prototype-as-the-ux-record). It freezes the selected interaction model for the real Expo app. It does not claim that the throwaway app implements current accounting, persistence, authentication, or native intake.

[SEN-30](https://linear.app/sennkai-labs/issue/SEN-30/build-lukapps-ui-core-and-backend-core) makes Expo the first production client for the personal iOS and Android cutover. Personal and Household Workspaces each have one Owner. Business activation, invitations, and collaboration are deferred. GAS remains authoritative until a later reconciled cutover. The current Linear maps, [Finance](../../CONTEXT.md), [context map](../../CONTEXT-MAP.md), and their ADRs govern implementation ahead of the old web-first migration sequence.

Read [DESIGN-CONTRACT.md](./DESIGN-CONTRACT.md) for executable tokens and shared components, and [CAPABILITY-CEILING.md](./CAPABILITY-CEILING.md) for the frozen-tree stop line.

## Home and the level-one door

Essential-first E is the selected Home. The month amount and story lead, then the available destinations. The user icon opens user and Workspace, with a normal back action, title, then content. Appearance is an open section on that page. Required actions remain visible; secondary explanations open on demand.

At level zero, show Drafts, the month story, and transaction access. Personal Workspace and the one pool named My money stay silent. Do not expose account management, Workspace-kind selection, Budget, Alerts, Recovery, or Month Close during first-run. The August 23 SEN-6 checkpoint includes Overview on this home; the August 23 SEN-22 first-run record and the current root `docs/experience-levels.md` reference keep level-zero transaction and month views without a separate Overview tile. This contract follows the first-run-specific boundary.

The level-one Workspace door is C, Look then ask. Adding a second place or creating a Budget opens that door deliberately. Ask only the additional facts needed for the chosen action. Places is a separate view, with name, amount, and a supporting receipt; product type belongs in detail. Adding a place uses a sheet and the same account setup from the posting account picker. Tapping the Home amount opens the action choice; the off-center plus control is retired. Alerts and Budget become available with level one. Recovery and Month Close remain level-two capabilities. When those capabilities are available, Plans opens Budget and Savings Recovery as siblings; Overview opens balances, activity, and Financial Account Profiles.

## Drafts and Month Close

D, Priority, simplified, is the selected inbox. Rows contain description, one supporting fact, and amount, then open review for the rest. Priority combines expiring Candidates and Candidates needing review. Ready Candidates can be confirmed together. The settled August 28 checkpoint changes the stacked Needs you now and Can wait groups into one segmented list with counts on its badges. Show one list at a time.

Home keeps the Drafts tile when the queue is empty. Month Close is an explicit footer action on the inbox when the capability is available, with a clear empty-state route into the review. It must never depend on a long press or a renamed Home tile. Completing a month stays unavailable while Local Entry Drafts dated in that month remain on this device. Remaining Notification Intake Candidates produce a warning, not a block. Completing calls `lockAccountingPeriod`; it creates no Journal Entry and changes no balance.

The old README, design principles, workflow `primaryLabel`, [SEN-51](https://linear.app/sennkai-labs/issue/SEN-51), and part of [SEN-49](https://linear.app/sennkai-labs/issue/SEN-49) describe Drafts changing to Complete month or a month-complete tile. The explicit [SEN-6](https://linear.app/sennkai-labs/issue/SEN-6/prototype-the-first-cutover-mobile-workflow) checkpoint of 2026-08-28, comment `1d678bfc-4eb6-4193-8651-9924fc66a50d`, settles the persistent Drafts tile and inbox footer. Preserve the visibility principle through that footer. Show the completed period state in Month Close.

Drafts is a navigation label, not permission to merge local record types. A manual Local Entry Draft holds incomplete expense, income, or Account Transfer facts. A Notification Intake Candidate comes from local capture and stays in its own encrypted device store. Neither syncs, becomes a Workspace Draft, or grants Posting Authority. Both expire after at most 15 days. Submitting a Local Entry Draft calls the ordinary online command with its local id as the idempotency key, then removes it only after success. Confirming a Ready Candidate calls the intake confirmation path and creates a Posted entry plus an immutable Receipt, without first entering the Drafts store. A Draft Savings Recovery Plan is a separate planning proposal.

Authority: [SEN-39](https://linear.app/sennkai-labs/issue/SEN-39/decide-the-expo-stores-and-shared-primitives), resolution `1a1e5754-2528-48b0-ad6a-2e18e64451c2` of 2026-09-30 explicitly corrects the earlier claim that a confirmed Candidate enters Drafts. [SEN-41](https://linear.app/sennkai-labs/issue/SEN-41/decide-when-a-command-drafts-instead-of-posting), resolution `3ff76ef4-5e39-4b6f-b73d-e1f2b647c23a`, and [ADR-0089 as resolved in SEN-41](https://linear.app/sennkai-labs/issue/SEN-41/decide-when-a-command-drafts-instead-of-posting) rule out a server Draft command or table. [SEN-45](https://linear.app/sennkai-labs/issue/SEN-45/specify-the-drafts-module) owns manual Draft review.

## Notification review and posting

[SEN-7](https://linear.app/sennkai-labs/issue/SEN-7), resolution `24ed5c3f-c53c-4995-865e-76d88cce87e2` of 2026-08-21, selects D's quiet grouped rows, a persistent unread reconciliation marker with its own explanation, content-free discard label and count, and a Month Close warning grouped by spacing rather than a colored alert card. The frozen warning component still loops an animation; carry over the accepted persistent marker, not indefinite pulsing.

Consent requires disclosure, OS settings, an access and service-health check, then source selection. Returning from settings alone is not consent. The enabled view shows Android listener or iOS automation status and the latest actual local health check. The prototype's hardcoded Listening and just-now labels are fixtures, not native service evidence. Discard OTP and security content before storage. Keep incomplete-capture notices content-free. Show expiring-soon items during the final three days in-app.

[SEN-52](https://linear.app/sennkai-labs/issue/SEN-52/specify-notification-intake-confirmation-and-its-receipt), resolution `50177b80-8db0-4c72-886c-e6225855494b` and correction `c6af8f4a-ed25-4fc1-a511-5de79f59ae17` of 2026-09-30, supplies the real confirmation contract. Cash expense and cash income accept Receipts. Confirm in batches of at most 50 with per-Candidate results, remove successes, and retain failures for review. The Receipt excludes raw text, source-app identity, OS/device identifiers, and content fingerprints. Use [ADR-0079 as settled in SEN-7](https://linear.app/sennkai-labs/issue/SEN-7) for that boundary.

The August 28 posting checkpoint uses one form with a signed amount, type choices, description, then account, transfer destination when needed, category, date, and Post. There is no separate In/Out control. A Candidate opens this form prefilled. One and Batch share the form; Batch keeps the selected D stack treatment. The small Posted banner is the later posting-specific confirmation choice from SEN-6 comment `1d678bfc-4eb6-4193-8651-9924fc66a50d`. [SEN-45](https://linear.app/sennkai-labs/issue/SEN-45/specify-the-drafts-module) specifies `ConfirmationScreen` for the Local Entry Draft flow. F02 should reuse common confirmation content for both presentations as an implementation adjustment; do not add a competing posting editor. Replacing the screen presentation with a banner everywhere would be a new implementation adjustment, not an already settled decision.

## First-run and account setup

First-run is D, one sheet with four distinct answers in the recorded sequence: main currency, optional purchase notifications, 8 to 12 starter spend categories plus add-your-own, and one starting amount, what you have right now. Create Personal and My money silently. Declining notifications leaves manual entry available. Finish shows a calm confirmation and lands on level-zero Home. Cancel abandons the preview.

[SEN-22](https://linear.app/sennkai-labs/issue/SEN-22) settles this sequence in its checked acceptance criteria and August 23 comments. Its older comment `9c0ac2d0-3603-4e9a-b855-6058b08a18be` and the frozen workflow ask Workspace kind, currency, then first account. [SEN-46](https://linear.app/sennkai-labs/issue/SEN-46/specify-the-onboarding-and-financial-account-setup-module) repeats that older sequence. It is superseded for first-run only; Household remains within first-cutover scope, with kind and account questions behind deliberate Workspace/account setup. The later SEN-22 request for more than one currency does not settle a second first-run flow. Keep one Functional Currency answer and leave additional currency behavior to the real account and command contracts.

Entered starting amounts become accounting through Start Tracking, not a mutable `Account.balance`. [SEN-40](https://linear.app/sennkai-labs/issue/SEN-40/specify-the-start-tracking-opening-entry), resolution `84e69f16-c14c-4633-b1eb-916f195c3798` of 2026-09-30, uses one mutation for profile creation and `recordOpeningPosition`. Zero posts nothing. Asset amounts mean held; card and loan amounts mean owed. The backend enforces opening dates and one active opening entry. Category creation and restyling reuse the same sheet in setup and posting, as SEN-22 comment `16e22431-2926-4dcb-9a70-c35a7f24467b` requires. The prototype's Transfer category does not carry over; [SEN-60](https://linear.app/sennkai-labs/issue/SEN-60) routes money-kept and debt answers through the account door.

## Plans and Posted activity

Plans is a door onto Budget and Savings Recovery, with separate destinations and stores. [SEN-17](https://linear.app/sennkai-labs/issue/SEN-17) requires those siblings. Its August 23 and August 28 comments `e36d04f2-f3c6-4efc-b53e-9000ddce0cd0` and `bde0fe56-ca69-400d-beaf-59d6198f6b2b` explicitly say the Board-only prototype does not yet implement that split. Preserve the Board's remaining-amount fill and danger wash without fill when over. Do not promote its limited routing into the production navigation contract. [SEN-43](https://linear.app/sennkai-labs/issue/SEN-43/decide-whether-budget-is-backend-state-or-device-state), resolution `3057898d-4b5f-48ef-8510-a4d880d9deea` of 2026-09-30, puts Budget in first cutover as backend Workspace state under Plans. It reads Posted expense lines by month and cannot block posting or change accounting.

Recovery reads plans and contributions. Move money posts a real Account Transfer before recording a contribution. Recovery creates neither a self-debt nor an expense reversal, and it owns no duplicate balances. A reversed supporting transfer stops counting toward progress. See [Savings Recovery](../../src/savingsRecovery/CONTEXT.md), [Budget context decision](https://linear.app/sennkai-labs/issue/SEN-43/decide-whether-budget-is-backend-state-or-device-state), and [SEN-39](https://linear.app/sennkai-labs/issue/SEN-39/decide-the-expo-stores-and-shared-primitives).

Posted activity is the shared ledger read for Home spending, Overview, account detail, and history. Overview takes four recent rows; account detail takes at most three; history loads 50 postings per page through one paginated query per list. Rows use title, supporting date and category or To/From account, then signed amount. Liability balances say owed. Basic views expose no debits, credits, or account codes.

A reversed original appears once, marked Reversed. Hide its reversal row in the basic activity list while retaining both accounting records and their effects in balances. This is a presentation rule, not ledger deletion or a new accounting status. Authority: [SEN-44](https://linear.app/sennkai-labs/issue/SEN-44/specify-the-posted-activity-module), resolution `81b965a2-724f-4b41-9585-d4aa8c3120c1` of 2026-09-30 and preceding decision `689414ab-8a86-4982-baab-869da2c81d32`.

## Real-module handoff

F02 builds [SEN-33](https://linear.app/sennkai-labs/issue/SEN-33/stand-up-the-expo-ui-core) in `apps/mobile`, consuming `src/theme/tokens.ts` directly. F01 supplies contracts and the pure token module only. It does not create routes, runtime packages, fonts, stores, or rendered components.

[SEN-37](https://linear.app/sennkai-labs/issue/SEN-37/wayfind-the-expo-ui-module-spec) and the settled SEN-39 resolution give Posted activity, Local Entry Drafts, Budget, and Savings Recovery one owner each. Candidates retain a separate encrypted device owner. Alerts is derived from Drafts, Recovery due, and month readiness. Workspace and profiles are backend reads, Theme is a device preference, and navigation belongs to the router. Month Close reads the owners and period status rather than copying them.

Build shared components once and use them across destinations. Prototype-local duplicated headers, buttons, choices, account balances, hardcoded counts, and stack state are evidence of shape only. A future dev-only preference lab belongs in this real app and exercises the real Finance domain. Budget rollover and computed versus entered Available Recovery Cash remain reviewable preferences. The lab cannot switch the settled Home/inbox/door layout or extend the frozen prototype. The exact sheet mechanism is also an implementation choice; the SEN-22 August 23 sheet comment explicitly limits its package choice to the Expo Go prototype.

Verification for this record establishes source provenance, token export equivalence, and standalone typing. It supplies no live UI, simulator, physical-device, native-intake, or accounting-cutover proof. F02 must prove fonts, shared component behavior, navigation, density, and touch targets in the real app. Parent review and integration remain separate gates.
